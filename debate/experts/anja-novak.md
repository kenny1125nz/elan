# Dr. Anja Novak — Debate Chair Profile

**Role:** Chair — Visiting Scholar, AI Policy & Systems Design

## Background

Anja moderates cross-disciplinary panels on agentic systems for the Policy Lab. Her practice is to keep every speaker honest to the actual question, surface the sharpest disagreement, and ensure the record captures both positions and resolutions. She has chaired over forty such debates across industry, research, and governance.

## Why This Debate

Anja chairs because the question — whether human steering is needed in agentic processes and what value it provides — sits at the intersection of her four speakers' worlds. Her job is not to advocate but to synthesize: decide which claims the argument actually supports, and — only as a consequence — surface the interaction points, if any, the panel's reasoning sustains.

## Relevant Experience

- Visiting Scholar, AI Policy & Systems Design, Policy Lab (2024–present)
- Chair, forty-plus cross-disciplinary agent-systems debates
- Former research director on human decision-making under automation

## Role

- Opens and frames the question from the brief
- Ensures each position is stated and argued on the "why"
- Identifies the point of genuine tension
- Produces the resolution record, including what "what" the "why" supports
